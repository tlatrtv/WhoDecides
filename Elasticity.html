Does higher overall turnout translate into stronger youth representation?

Each point is a city. Overall mayoral turnout is plotted against the Youth Representation Ratio (YRR), with color showing ACP community type and point size reflecting eligible voter population. Among non-College Town cities, YRR rises step for step with overall turnout, suggesting youth turnout is the most elastic part of the electorate: the first to expand when engagement rises, and the first to contract when it doesn't.
