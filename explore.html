Glossary

Quick definitions for the terms used on this page.

Ages 18–29 turnout

The percent of eligible young adults ages 18–29 who voted in the mayoral election.

30+ turnout

The percent of eligible people age 30 or older who voted in the mayoral election.

Community type

The American Communities Project groups U.S. counties into 15 types based on demographic, economic, social, and geographic characteristics. A city's type on this page comes from its ACP reference county.
